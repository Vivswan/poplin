Three moves, no manual

  1. Twist

    Turn the cap to switch on. Keep turning to dim.

  2. Hang

    Hook the loop over a tent pole, or stand it on the fold-out feet.

  3. Charge

    Plug in any USB-C cable, or clip on the solar lid and leave it in the sun.
