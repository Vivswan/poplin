What happened

The enamel teapot range launched in the second week of July and sold through its first run in nineteen days. That one range accounts for most of the teaware gain.

Coast and Islands both ran out of the 1.5 litre kettle in August. Orders were there; stock was not. The second kiln line comes on in November.

Online direct is still the smallest channel and the fastest growing. Its average order is $71, well above the shop average.

Watch next quarter

  • Whether teapot buyers come back for refills within ninety days.
