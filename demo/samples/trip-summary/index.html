Four days on the Saltmere Coast

Two travellers, three stops, one ferry. Everything below is booked and paid for except dinners and cafes.

  1. Port Hallow Two nights by the harbour
  2. Greywick A day trip by coast bus
  3. Tern Island One night, by ferry
  4. Port Hallow The train home

Good to know

Tickets
Train, ferry, and tour tickets are saved to the phone. The three booking numbers are in the timeline.
Tides
The tide-pool walk only runs near low water. If the 08:50 bus is missed, the walk is missed.
Pack
Waterproof jacket, shoes that can get wet, a dry bag for the kayak, and cash for the island cafe.
